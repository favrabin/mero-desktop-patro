About Mero Desktop Patro

Application philosophy, technical architecture, and open-source acknowledgments.

Mero Desktop Patro

Official Release 1.0.0

Mero Desktop Patro is a clean, distraction-free desktop calendar utility created for Microsoft Windows. It provides direct, instantaneous access to Bikram Sambat (वि.सं.) dates, public holidays, cultural festivals, and astronomical lunar Tithis without requiring a web browser or bloated software.

Designed with an emphasis on minimalism, legibility, and modern desktop ergonomics, Mero Desktop Patro stays quietly in your system tray and displays the date via a lightweight floating desktop widget that respects your screen space and daily workflow.

Privacy Policy

Official Privacy Statement • Effective as of Version 1.0.0 (October 2026)

1. Privacy by Architecture

Mero Desktop Patro is engineered from the ground up on a privacy-first, offline-first philosophy. We believe calendar utilities should respect your privacy unconditionally. The application does not collect, track, transmit, or monetize any personal information, usage metrics, or browsing habits.

2. Zero Telemetry & Zero Data Collection

The software operates 100% locally on your computer. When you open the widget, check dates, or navigate months, no network requests are dispatched to any remote server. There are no tracking scripts, analytics SDKs, advertising beacons, or third-party monitoring tools embedded in the binary.

3. Local Preferences & Storage

To deliver a consistent user experience across restarts, Mero Desktop Patro saves only non-identifying operational preferences in your local user app directory (%APPDATA%/...):

  • Your desktop widget screen coordinates (X, Y) so the widget reopens where you placed it.
  • Your UI preferences (e.g. chosen size preset, surface theme, autostart toggle).

These configuration files reside exclusively on your machine and are never transmitted externally.

4. External Links

The application contains links to the developer's official portfolio website (portfolio.rabinthapa9.com.np). These external links only open when you explicitly click on them. When opened, your default web browser handles the connection according to standard internet protocols.

5. Operating System Integrations

If you enable the optional "Start with Windows" feature, the application registers an entry in your user account's standard Windows Startup registry key. No administrative elevation or root modifications are performed.

Important Information Regarding Calendar Calculations and Official Observances

1. Independent Development Declaration

Mero Desktop Patro is an independent software project developed by Rabin Thapa. It is NOT an official publication, application, or service of the Government of Nepal, the Nepal Panchanga Nirnayak Samiti, or any state-affiliated ministry.

2. Calendar Calculation & Conversion Accuracy

The Bikram Sambat (BS) calendar is a lunisolar system. Unlike fixed algorithmic calendars, official month lengths (varying between 29 and 32 days) and public holiday declarations are finalized by Nepalese authorities periodically.

While Mero Desktop Patro incorporates verified astronomical datasets and passes rigorous automated verification testing across BS 2000 through BS 2090, users must verify dates with the official Nepal Rajpatra (Nepal Gazette) published by the Ministry of Home Affairs for critical banking deadlines, legal transactions, court hearings, and government paperwork.

3. Religious Festivals & Lunar Tithis

Astronomical Tithis (such as Ekadashi, Purnima, and Aaunshi) are calculated based on standard Nepalese planetary algorithms. Specific religious observances, localized vratas, or muhurats may vary slightly based on local sunrise times (Surya Udaya) or regional customs. Consult your family Jyotish or local Panchanga for ritualistic timings.
